# Code of conduct

EACP adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews and any other project space.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks
and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on the
[@atipongsena](https://github.com/atipongsena) GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Please do not
report conduct issues in public issues or pull requests.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
